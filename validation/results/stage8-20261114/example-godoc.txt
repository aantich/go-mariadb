func RecordOrder(ctx context.Context, db *sql.DB, operationID string, payload []byte) error
    RecordOrder atomically stores an operation and its outbox record. The
    schema must give operation_id a unique key. A failed Commit is an uncertain
    outcome: callers reconcile that ID through a fresh connection before
    deciding to retry.
